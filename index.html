Learn More About Us

Voltex is a leading provider of cutting-edge energy solutions, powering the future with innovative technology. Founded in 2024, we are developing smart insoles that harness the energy generated from everyday footsteps and convert it into usable electrical power through piezoelectric technology.

Our vision is to transform the way we think about everyday movement. By integrating energy-harvesting technology into footwear, we aim to turn every step into an opportunity to generate clean, renewable energy while maintaining comfort and practicality.

We are driven by a commitment to innovation, sustainability, and purposeful engineering. As we continue to develop our technology, our goal is to create solutions that are not only technologically exciting, but also practical, accessible, and capable of contributing to a more sustainable future for generations to come.

Why Choose Voltex Insoles

As a leading provider of renewable energy solutions, Voltex offers a compelling set of benefits that set us apart from the competition. When you choose Voltex, you're not just investing in industry-leading products - you're partnering with a company that is dedicated to powering a brighter, more sustainable future.

Innovative Energy Harvesting

We capture the kinetic impact from every step you take and turn it directly into usable electricity through built-in piezoelectric technology, turning your ordinary daily walks into a personal and continuous power grid.

Sustainable by Design

Generating clean, renewable energy should not require extra effort. We build power generation directly into footwear you wear every day, reducing your reliance on traditional wall outlets seamlessly and effortlessly over time.

Real-World Movement Focus

There are no new habits to learn, complex instructions to follow, or bulky extra gadgets to carry around. Just slip our smart insoles into your shoes, go about your normal day, and let your natural stride do all the heavy lifting.
